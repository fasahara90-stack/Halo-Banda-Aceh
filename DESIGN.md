## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a welcoming, vibrant, and civic-educational digital gateway for the historic capital of Aceh. Rebranding from "Charming Banda Aceh" to "HALO BANDA ACEH", the identity balances proud provincial heritage with lively digital accessibility. The tone is festive, informative, resilient, and inclusive—serving schoolchildren on study tours, domestic and international cultural explorers, and resident citizens seeking local updates.

Visually, the system combines **High-Contrast Modernism** with authentic regional ornamentation. Architectural geometries inspired by the Pinto Aceh motif, the rhythmic lattice of the Aceh Tsunami Museum, and the iconic white and obsidian domes of the Baiturrahman Grand Mosque inform the graphic structures. Rather than a dusty historical artifact, the interface behaves like an energized city pass: punchy sector badges, warm gamified learning modules, crisp ticket-grade QR codes, and sensory audio-visual feedback loops.

## Colors

The palette takes inspiration directly from traditional Acehnese ceremonial regalia and local architectural landmarks:
- **Aceh Gold (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`)**: The primary brand driver, symbolizing prestige, warm hospitality, and the royal lineage of the Sultanate.
- **Emerald Green (`#059669` / `#10B981`)**: The secondary anchor, representing Islamic heritage, serenity, and lush natural beauty.
- **Royal Ruby Red (`#DC2626` / `#EF4444`)**: An energetic accent evoking courage, rich culinary spices (Mie Aceh), and historical resilience.
- **Ocean Sapphire Blue (`#0284C7` / `#0EA5E9`)**: Reflecting the coastal identity of the Malacca Strait, marine conservation, and civic infrastructure.
- **Charcoal Neutral (`#0F172A` to `#1E293B`)**: Echoes the black domes of Baiturrahman and dark titanium cladding of the Tsunami Museum, offering commanding typographic contrast against clean porcelain white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces.

### Tourism Sector Badges (Semantic Mapping)
Each primary tourism pillar is strictly mapped to a distinct visual badge token:
- **Wisata Alam (Nature & Eco-Tourism)**: Emerald Green (`#059669`) with mint-tinted fill (`#ECFDF5`).
- **Heritage & Sejarah (History)**: Aceh Gold (`#D97706`) with warm amber tint (`#FFFBEB`).
- **Tsunami Memorial**: Ocean Sapphire Blue (`#0284C7`) with seafoam tint (`#F0F9FF`).
- **Wisata Kuliner (Culinary Delights)**: Royal Ruby Red (`#DC2626`) with spiced rose tint (`#FEF2F2`).
- **Wisata Religi (Spiritual & Islamic Culture)**: Deep Teal Indigo (`#0D9488`) with celadon tint (`#F0FDFA`).

## Typography

The type system blends structural geometric punch with friendly, high-legibility sans text:
- **Display & Headlines (`epilogue`)**: Chosen for its sturdy, expressive geometric rhythm. It gives banners, attraction headers, and gamified achievements a confident, headline-worthy presence reminiscent of modern cultural exhibitions.
- **Body & Controls (`plusJakartaSans`)**: Built for fluid reading across screens. Provides crisp rendering in dense tourist agendas, bilingual Indonesian-Acehnese-English cultural descriptions, and quiz prompts.

All display titles use negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to achieve modern poster-like impact. Badges and categorical labels enforce uppercase treatment with generous positive tracking (`0.06em`) to ensure instant legibility over photography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp ambient lighting mixed with subtle regional graphic underlays rather than heavy artificial shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) and ultra-soft sand-tinted alabaster (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest)**: Elevated with a gentle ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`, encased in a delicate outline (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards)**: Elevated to `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`, shifting border color toward the primary gold tone.
- **Level 3 (Modal / QR Code Overlays / Quiz Sound Prompts)**: Deep focused elevation `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)` over a 40% blurred backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- **Aceh Pattern Lattice Insets**: Cultural cards may feature a watermark vector of the Pinto Aceh or Tsunami Museum lattice at 4%–6% opacity within the container surface, providing tactile sense without compromising text contrast.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (medium-high rounded architecture):
- Standard interactive controls, form elements, and action cards use `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius.
- Feature hero cards, quiz containers, and attraction cards scale to `1rem` (`16px`).
- Badges, sector chips, and pill navigation buttons utilize full rounded caps (`9999px`) to maintain a friendly, approachable aesthetic for students and family visitors.
- QR display windows remain framed within rounded-xl viewfinders featuring custom corner brackets reminiscent of traditional Acehnese archways.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Aceh Gold)**: `#D97706` background with white text, bold font-weight (`label-lg`), and slight hover lift. Active click scales down (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action (Emerald Green)**: `#059669` fill, utilized for discovery, audio narration playback, and confirmation flows.
- **Ghost / Cultural Outline**: Transparent background, 2px border in `#D97706` or `#0F172A`, paired with icon glyphs.

### 2. Tourism Sector Badges & Chips
- Compact pills styled according to sector token colors. Each chip displays the sector's iconic symbol alongside bold uppercase text (e.g., green palm leaf for *Wisata Alam*, red bowl for *Wisata Kuliner*).
- Active chips have a solid color fill; inactive chips sit on an 8% background tint with a 1px matching border.

### 3. Cards
- **Destination Card**: Full bleed 16:9 photographic top, sticky sector badge at the top right, headline-sm title, walking/driving distance metric, and audio tour indicator.
- **Feedback & Sound Cards (Gamified Quiz)**: Interactive stateful cards with distinct border feedback:
  - Default: White card, subtle border.
  - Selected: Aceh Gold ring (`2px solid #D97706`).
  - Correct Answer: Emerald Green fill (`#ECFDF5`), green badge, positive chime audio cue indicator.
  - Incorrect Answer: Ruby Red shake state (`#FEF2F2`), red feedback banner.

### 4. Interactive Gamified Quiz Modules
- Stepper progress bar rendered in Aceh Gold gradient.
- Live score pill counter pinned to the header.
- Multi-choice option panels with large tap targets (minimum 54px touch height) and instant celebratory visual micro-animations.

### 5. QR Code Passport Pass
- Crisp white substrate container flanked with Pinto Aceh framing motifs.
- High-contrast black QR matrix with the official Banda Aceh emblem rendered at its center.
- Integrated quick-action buttons: "Simpan ke Galeri" (Save) and "Tunjukkan Petugas" (Show to Attendant).

### 6. Inputs & Selectors
- Search bar with quick filter pills underneath. Inputs feature 44px minimum height, 1px neutral-gray border, expanding to a 2px amber halo on focus (`#D9770633`).
